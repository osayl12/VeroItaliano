## The eight topics (authored decisions)
1. **Vibe:** warm, smoky, after-dark, hand-made, local.
   References: the night-kitchen scenes in *Chef* (2014); the *Chef's Table* opening titles (slow fire, macro texture); a family shop in a Galilee village with the lights on after sunset.
2. **Journey:** Dusk and the oven being lit → the dough in his hands → tonight's menu laid out on the table → a hush while one pizza goes into the fire → **the slice comes out** → tonight's deals → the oven is hot until closing, call now.
3. **Energy:** calm opening, a building middle, then **silence just before the peak**. The peak is loud, and the close is calm and certain.
4. **Feeling and the moment:** see the curve below. The one moment is the cheese pull.
5. **No site you've seen does this:** your scroll physically pulls a slice out of the pie. The cheese stretches with your hand and snaps.
6. **Range:** warm-editorial with a touch of retro trattoria (bold Hebrew and Arabic display type, wood and cream, flag accents). It does **not** use the gray, airy "premium-minimal" look.
7. **Structure:** **distinct scenes**, not one unbroken world. Each chapter is a different moment of one evening, joined by cuts.
8. **Assets:** real phone footage from Sharif is first choice (shot list in PLAN.md). Logo and flyers already exist. Generated photoreal assets are only a fallback, and they must be photographic. No clay or cartoon diorama.

## Feeling curve (one line per act; emotion, then what causes it)
1. **Warm curiosity:** the oven fire glows behind the headline, and flour and embers drift in front of it.
2. **Trust:** real hands stretching real dough, slowed down under your scroll.
3. **Appetite:** a long wooden table of tonight's pizzas slides sideways past you, with real prices.
4. **Hush / anticipation** (*authored silence*): almost black. One pizza goes into the fire. Very little text.
5. **PEAK, delight:** your scroll lifts a hot slice out of the pie, and the cheese stretches until it snaps.
6. **Playful urgency:** tonight's deals land on the table like plates, with live countdowns.
7. **Certainty:** the shop's real live status ("the oven is hot until 23:00 tonight") and two big buttons. The page stops and holds.

## The peak
> "You scroll and it pulls a slice out of the pizza, and the cheese actually stretches until it snaps."

It lives in **Act 5**. It gets the largest span on the page, and Act 4 (the hush) is deliberately quieter than it.
